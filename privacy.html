Privacy Policy

Daily Places Now · Last updated 7 October 2026

What this app is

"Daily Places Now" is a private automation used only by the owner of the Daily Places Now YouTube channel. It creates short videos from public webcams and uploads them to that one channel.

Google user data

The app requests a single permission, youtube.upload, from the channel owner's own Google account. It uses it only to upload videos to that channel. It does not read, store, share or sell any other Google user data, and it is not offered to other users.

Visitors and viewers

This website and the app do not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and do not collect personal information from visitors or viewers. Watching the videos on YouTube is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Camera images

Videos are made from webcams run by public agencies and organisations whose terms allow reuse. If you believe an image shows something it shouldn't, contact us and we will remove it.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: leave a comment on the YouTube channel.
